This video explains the dealership admin: the page where the catalogue behind the configurator is managed. That means the cars, the wheels with their sizes and prices, the finishes, the paints, and the enquiries customers send. It lives at slash admin, and it starts with a sign-in form. You sign in with an email and a password, and only emails on the allowlist are let through. An allowlist is simply a list of addresses the system trusts. If it's your first time, or you've forgotten your password, the link underneath emails you a one-time link to set a new one.
