Step two is the wheels. The camera moves in on the wheel, and the list shows the real stock: six designs, each with its name and a starting price. The second line tells you how the wheel is built. A monoblock is machined from a single piece of forged aluminium. Two-piece and three-piece wheels are assembled from separate parts: a face, and one or two barrel sections. I'll choose the F F one, a two-piece wheel with a split five-spoke face.
